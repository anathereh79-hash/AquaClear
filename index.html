AquaClear skims the surface by itself.

A micro-vessel airboat with a printed hull and a ducted fan. It sweeps the water surface and catches debris in a front net before it sinks.

  • Moves

    BLDC propeller, servo rudder

  • Collects

    Front-net skimming

  • Steers

    Three ultrasonic sensors

Steering

Three sensors feed an Arduino on the custom board, which sets the BLDC thrust and the rudder servo. It slows under 50 cm ahead and goes straight while both sides have more than 15 cm.

The cleaning cycle runs in four steps.

  • 1. Skim

    The robot patrols the surface and collects debris.

    Patrol
  • 2. Detect a full net

    Onboard sensors notice when the net is full.

    Sense
  • 3. Return to base

    It navigates back to the base station.

    Dock
  • 4. Dump and recharge

    The station empties the net and charges the unit.

    Reset

Steps 2 to 4 are the full system design. The verified prototype covers navigation and obstacle avoidance.

Hardware and electronics. Printed hull, brushless fan, custom board.

  • Hull

    PLA, printed from a Fusion 360 model for buoyancy and low drag. Black hull, watertight white rim.

  • Propulsion

    BLDC motor in a yellow safety duct.

  • Steering

    Servo-driven rudder for tight pool corners.

  • Sensing

    Three ultrasonic sensors: left, ahead, right.

  • Control

    Arduino on a custom Autodesk Eagle board with isolated VIN, BLV and 5V rails.

  • Design

    Parametric model in Fusion 360.

    Step 1
  • Slice

    Tuned for fast, low-waste printing.

    Step 2
  • Assemble

    Printed PLA hull and white deck rim.

    Step 3

Two modes: autonomous and manual.

  • Autonomous

    The sensors read left, ahead and right. The robot slows within 50 cm of an obstacle and steers toward the wider side.

  • Manual

    Send one letter over the serial connection and the robot obeys at once. Try the keys.

Commands: F, B, S, L, R
